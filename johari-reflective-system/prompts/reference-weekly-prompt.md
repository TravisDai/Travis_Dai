# Reference weekly prompt

Copy the prompt below into a conversational GenAI tool, then provide your de-identified teaching context or artefacts.

---

I would like you to support a structured reflection on my teaching using four Johari-inspired interaction functions.

Use **only the material and context I provide** as the basis for claims about this teaching situation. Distinguish clearly between evidence, interpretation, and suggestions. Treat your output as candidate reflective material for my review, not as an authoritative judgement.

## Open — consolidation

Consolidate my articulated strengths, challenges, decisions, constraints, and relevant evidence from the supplied material.

Provide a concise current-state summary.

## Blind — critique / alternative interpretation

Identify a plausible omission, misalignment, or alternative interpretation in the supplied material.

For each important issue:
- explain the possible impact; and
- suggest a practical response I could consider.

Present these as hypotheses or alternative interpretations, not as facts about me.

## Hidden — assumption elicitation

Ask focused questions that help me articulate assumptions, constraints, rationales, or heuristics that may currently be implicit.

After the questions, briefly synthesise the assumptions or constraints that become explicit from my answers. If I have not answered yet, do not invent them.

## Unknown — ideation

Propose context-sensitive instructional possibilities that I could consider.

For each candidate action:
- explain the rationale;
- state any important implementation condition; and
- give an observable indicator I could use later to review a trial.

Do not describe an idea as adopted, successful, or effective unless I provide later evidence that supports that conclusion.

## Final educator-review summary

Finish with a short section titled **For my review** that separates:
1. evidence already present in my material;
2. interpretations or hypotheses for me to check; and
3. candidate actions I may accept, modify, reject, or defer.

Do not infer or request unnecessary personal information about students.
