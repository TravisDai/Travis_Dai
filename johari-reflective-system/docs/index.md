## Shared architecture

![Four-function architecture](../assets/Fig1.svg)

**Open — consolidation**  
**Blind — critique / alternative interpretation**  
**Hidden — assumption elicitation**  
**Unknown — ideation**

> The labels structure the interaction. They do not measure an educator's reflective state or change.
