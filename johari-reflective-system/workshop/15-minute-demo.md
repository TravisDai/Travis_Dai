# 15-minute demonstration

A short format for showing colleagues how the four-function system works.

## 0–2 min — frame the system

Explain two points:

1. Open, Blind, Hidden, and Unknown are **interaction functions**, not measured reflective states.
2. AI output is candidate material. The educator reviews every interpretation and action.

## 2–4 min — choose a small teaching case

Use one short, de-identified example, such as:
- a lab instruction students found confusing;
- a common assessment error;
- a lesson activity that did not work as expected.

## 4–9 min — run the reference prompt

Paste the [reference weekly prompt](../prompts/reference-weekly-prompt.md) and the short teaching context.

Show the four sections as they are generated.

## 9–12 min — model human review

Choose one item from Blind or Unknown and ask:
- What evidence supports this?
- What is interpretation rather than evidence?
- Would I accept, modify, or reject it?
- What would I observe if I tried the suggestion?

## 12–15 min — record one decision

Use the [weekly reflection log](../templates/weekly-reflection-log.md) to record one selected insight or candidate action.

End by reminding participants that completing four sections demonstrates workflow operation, not improvement in reflection.
